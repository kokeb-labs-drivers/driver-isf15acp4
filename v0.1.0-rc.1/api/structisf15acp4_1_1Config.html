Detailed Description

Everything the driver sends at start-up, and its own time knobs.

Config{} is the ISF15ACP4 (NKK Table 3). The controller values are the module's table values (contrast, offset, multiplex ratio, master configuration, phase periods, clock, gray scale, pre-charge, VCOMH): NKK Note 1 says changing them may damage the OLED module. The dim contrasts may not exceed the contrasts. The time knobs are the driver's own and document an open point of the datasheets (docs/ambiguities.md). It has no operator==.
