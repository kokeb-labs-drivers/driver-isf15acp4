Detailed Description

Debounces a switch sampled at any rate and classifies the press.

A level change is accepted once the new level has been seen continuously for debounce_ms (a sample of the old level in between starts the wait again). Pressed and Released carry the time of the first sample of the new level, so the debounce delay is not in the timestamp. Poll at least twice per debounce time: a press shorter than the poll period is not seen. One event is returned per Update(); a second condition that is due stays due and is returned by the next call.
